Introduction

Artificial Intelligence is an important technology used in education, research and many other fields. This Faculty Development Programme introduces faculty members to AI tools.

Objectives

  1. Introduce AI concepts and applications.
  2. Demonstrate AI tools for academic work.
  3. Explore AI-assisted content creation.
  4. Discuss ethical and responsible AI usage.

Expected Outcomes

Participants will gain basic knowledge of AI tools and understand how they can support teaching and research.
